## Key Concepts

### Statistics
Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data.

### Distribution
A distribution shows how data values are spread across a range and helps reveal patterns such as normality or skewness.

### Correlation
Correlation indicates whether two variables move together and how strongly they are related.

### Hypothesis Testing
Hypothesis testing is used to determine whether an observed result is statistically significant or due to random chance.
